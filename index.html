THE COLOPHON

Creating at the intersection of curatorial discipline and spontaneous joy.

Over the past decade, I have operated as an independent creative director, visual strategist, and bookbinder between Paris, Barcelona, and Tokyo. My practice is grounded in the belief that digital artifacts should possess the same tactile resonance, surprise, and intimacy as a rare first-edition magazine.

Rather than adhering to sterile design conventions, every project begins with an inquiry into material culture: How does ink sink into handmade cotton paper? How does a single punctuation of cherry red awaken a field of soft cream? What happens when we allow a design to breathe?
